What's new in Community Voice AI

Version 4 (November 2026): three new features, added by the ByteBusters team at Yewa College, Ilaro, Ogun State. All of them work on a basic phone, need no account, and collect only what each feature needs.

📬 Letter tracker, upgraded

Every letter you send now has a status you can change: Sent, Acknowledged or Resolved. Choose when to be reminded (7, 14, 21 or 30 days) and add the reminder to your phone's calendar with one tap (an .ics file). A one-tap follow-up draft is written on your phone with no internet and no AI, using the date, office and reference you saved. You can also add letters you wrote on paper or somewhere else.

Your data: Saved on your phone only. Nothing is sent to our server.

Try it

✍️ Petitions with signatures

Turn any saved letter into a petition. Neighbours add only a first name and an area: either on their own phones with a link you share on WhatsApp, or by passing your phone around. Download the signature list as a PDF to attach to your letter, or print it. Rude words, surnames and numbers are refused; an online petition takes at most 500 signatures, one per name and area.

Your data: Online petitions store the title, request, letter (phone numbers and emails hidden) and each signer's first name and area for 180 days. The person who started it can delete it. "Collect on this phone" stores nothing on our server.

Try it

🏛️ Who to send it to (Ogun State)

Pick any of the 20 Local Government Areas in Ogun State and see the offices people usually write to: the Local Government Chairman, ward councillor, Ward Development Committee, LGEA, CDA, traditional ruler, state ministries, police (DPO, PCRC) and the electricity company (IBEDC customer care, then the NERC forum office). Each card shows how to address the letter and a verify locally note. We show office titles only and never guess phone numbers, emails or names; you can fill in details you have checked, and share a checked list with your class or CDA.

Your data: Details you type stay on your phone.
