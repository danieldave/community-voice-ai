About Community Voice AI: who built this

In short: a free tool that turns a community problem into a polite formal letter, made by secondary school students in the ByteBusters coding club at Yewa College, Ilaro, Ogun State, Nigeria. ByteBusters is run by Odu'a Investment Foundation.

Why we built it

Many communities live with broken boreholes, dark streets, bad roads and blocked drains, but people are not sure which office to write to or how to write a formal letter. We wanted a free tool that helps anyone, in everyday English or Pidgin, write a respectful letter and follow it up.

Who built it

A team of students at the Yewa College ByteBusters centre, including the Generative AI Team, planned the app, wrote the guides and coded the website, with guidance from the ByteBusters Centre Lead. The Nigerian Sign Language page was made with Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing students. Along the way we learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript, how to use an AI model safely, how to put a website online with Vercel, and how search engines read pages.

What it is, and is not

  • It is a free writing helper. You are always the author: the AI drafts, you check and edit every word.
  • It is not a government office and it does not deliver letters for you. Offices are organised differently in each state, so confirm the right office locally.
  • It is not for emergencies: call 112.
