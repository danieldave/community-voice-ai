Community Issue Map: what people are writing about

How it works: when someone writes a letter with Community Voice AI they can tick one box to add their problem to this map. We then count only the state and the problem type. We never receive the letter, the area or any words. Any number under 3 is hidden so nobody can be identified.

Why this matters

One letter is easy to ignore. A pattern is not. These counts show councillors, journalists, schools and community groups which problems people near them are raising, so that they can ask for the right fix.

You may quote these numbers with a link to this page. They show what users of this app chose to report, not every problem in the state.
