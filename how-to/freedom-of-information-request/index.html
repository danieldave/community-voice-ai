How do I make a Freedom of Information (FOI) request in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Under the Freedom of Information Act, anyone can write to a public institution asking for records, such as a project's contract sum. You do not have to say why, and the law gives the institution seven days to respond.

Step by step

  1. Address the letter to the head of the institution.
  2. Heading: "Request for information under the Freedom of Information Act".
  3. Describe exactly which record you want.
  4. Say how you want to receive it (copy, email).
  5. Get an acknowledgement copy and note the date.
  6. If refused or ignored, an adult or a civic organisation can help you take it further.
