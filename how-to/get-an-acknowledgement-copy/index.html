How do I prove that my letter was received?

Quick answer: Take two copies. Ask the receiving officer to stamp, sign and date your copy. That "acknowledgement copy" is your proof.

Step by step

  1. Print or write two identical copies.
  2. Deliver in office hours to the registry or secretary.
  3. Ask politely for a stamp, signature, name and date on your copy.
  4. If sent by email, keep the sent email and any reply.
  5. Record it in our My Letters tracker so you are reminded to follow up.
