How do I write a formal letter to a government office in Nigeria?

Quick answer: A formal letter has your address and date, the office's address, a salutation, a bold heading, three short paragraphs (the problem, the effect, the request), a polite close and your signature.

Step by step

  1. Top right: your address and the date.
  2. Left: the title and address of the person you are writing to.
  3. "Dear Sir" or "Dear Madam", then a heading in capitals that names the problem and place.
  4. Paragraph 1: what the problem is, where, since when.
  5. Paragraph 2: who it affects and how.
  6. Paragraph 3: exactly what you are asking for, and by when.
  7. "Yours faithfully", your signature and name, and "cc" for anyone copied.
