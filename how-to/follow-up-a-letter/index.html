What do I do if nobody replies to my letter?

Quick answer: Wait about two weeks, then send a short follow-up that quotes the date and reference of the first letter. If there is still no reply, go one level higher and copy the first office.

Step by step

  1. Check that the first letter was received: look at your stamped copy.
  2. After 14 days, write a polite follow-up quoting the first letter.
  3. Visit or call the office and ask who is handling it.
  4. After another two weeks, write to the next level (commissioner, assembly member, Public Complaints Commission) and attach both letters.
  5. Tell the community what has happened so far.
