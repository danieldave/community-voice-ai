How can students report a problem in their school or community?

Quick answer: Students can write a respectful letter through the class teacher, student council or principal, describe the problem with facts, and suggest a solution.

Step by step

  1. Talk to a teacher or parent first.
  2. Collect facts: what, where, since when, who is affected.
  3. Write the letter together as a class or club.
  4. Suggest something realistic.
  5. Say thank you when something is done: it makes the next request easier.
