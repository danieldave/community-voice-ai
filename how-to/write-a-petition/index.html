How do I write a community petition with signatures?

Quick answer: A petition is a formal letter signed by many people. Keep the request to one clear sentence and attach a signature sheet with names, streets and signatures.

Step by step

  1. Agree the request at a community meeting.
  2. Write the letter in calm, factual words.
  3. Add a signature sheet: number, name, street, signature. Do not collect phone numbers unless needed.
  4. Choose three people to deliver it and speak for the group.
  5. Keep a photocopy of everything.
