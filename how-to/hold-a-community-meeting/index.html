How do I organise a community meeting about a problem?

Quick answer: Invite people through leaders they trust, keep one topic, let everyone speak, write down what was agreed, and choose who will deliver the letter.

Step by step

  1. Tell the CDA chairman, religious leaders and youth leaders first.
  2. Pick a neutral place and a short time.
  3. One topic, one hour.
  4. Write minutes: date, number present, decisions.
  5. Read the letter aloud (our community-language copy helps) and collect signatures.
