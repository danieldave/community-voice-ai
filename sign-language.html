🤟 Learn to Sign

American Sign Language (ASL) words and fingerspelling for talking about problems in our community, so Deaf and hearing people can talk together. Everyone deserves a voice.

📘 These signs are American Sign Language (ASL). Nigerian Sign Language (NSL) grew from ASL. Andrew Foster brought it to Nigeria's first Deaf school in Ibadan in 1960, so many signs are the same. But NSL has grown in its own way, and some Nigerian signs are different. Always learn from Deaf teachers in your area.
