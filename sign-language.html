Nigerian Sign Language (NSL)

Words that help Deaf and hearing people talk together about problems in our community. Everyone deserves a voice.

Nigerian Sign Language differs from American Sign Language. Learn from Deaf teachers in your area.

How we make this page accurate

We do not draw or make up signs. Every sign on this page is added only after a qualified Nigerian Sign Language signer or Deaf teacher has recorded or checked it. Where we do not have a checked video yet, you will see “Video coming soon”.

Every video also has a written description, so no one needs sound to use this page.
