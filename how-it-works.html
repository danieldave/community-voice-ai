💡 How It Works

Simple steps, no sign-up, and your personal details never reach the AI.

The steps

  1. You fill the form. Pick the problem, your state and area, how long it has lasted, who is affected, who the letter is for, and the tone. Then describe it in your own words — English or Pidgin.
  2. The privacy filter cleans your text. Before anything leaves your phone, it removes phone numbers, emails, long ID or account numbers, and any name written after “my name is”.
  3. Your choices are joined into one prompt. Our server checks every choice, runs the privacy filter a second time, and sends the prompt to the AI.
  4. The AI returns a report as JSON. It contains the letter, an urgency level, safe steps your community can take, and the type of office to send it to.
  5. A conditional colours the urgency pill. High = red “Urgent”, medium = amber “Important”, low = green “Can wait”. The words always show with the colour.
  6. You review and edit. The letter appears in a box you can change. Copy it, download it, or print it. You are the author.

Your privacy

  • No sign-up, no database, no cookies. Nothing you type is saved on our server.
  • The filter runs in your browser first, and again on our server as a backup.
  • After each letter, open “See exactly what was sent to the AI” to check the cleaned text yourself.

Our guardrails

      
    
        
  • No names of people, and no blame or insults. The letter reports the problem, not a person.
    • 
        
  • A polite, respectful tone to officials — even when you choose “Firm and urgent”.
    • 
        
  • It suggests the kind of office to write to, never a made-up person.
    • 
        
  • It does not invent numbers, laws or facts. It only uses what you told it.
    • 
        
  • If there is danger to life, it reminds you to call 112 first.
    • 
        
  • Safe self-help steps only: it will never tell you to touch wires, enter floodwater or confront anyone.
