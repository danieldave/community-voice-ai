    How petitions stay safe
    
      
      
        
    • Signers give a first name and area only. Numbers, surnames and rude words are refused.
      • 
        
    • Online petitions accept at most 500 signatures, one per first name and area, and a limited number per hour from one connection. They are deleted after 180 days, or earlier by the person who started them.
      • 
        
    • Names are typed by the signers themselves and are not checked. Say so when you attach the list, and add a paper sheet with real signatures for important petitions. See how to write a petition.
