Who do I report not enough desks or chairs to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Ask the principal to write to the education authority with exact numbers. Old students' associations and local businesses often help with furniture.

Write toThe principal, then the education authority (LGEA and SUBEB for primary, State Ministry of Education for secondary).
If nothing happens, go toThe PTA and old students' association.

Collect this before you write

  • Pupils per class and seats per class.
  • Photos of a classroom (no faces).
  • Which classes are worst.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

In [class], [number] pupils share [number] desks. Many write on their laps or the floor. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
