Who do I report fallen or dangerous electric cables to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Treat fallen or sparking cables as an emergency: keep people away, call the distribution company's emergency line or 112, then follow up with a written report.

Write toYour electricity distribution company immediately. Keep everyone away. If life is in danger, call 112.
If nothing happens, go toThe Local Government Council and the electricity safety agency.

Collect this before you write

  • A photo from a safe distance.
  • Exact location.
  • When it was first seen.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

An electric cable has fallen at [location] and remains live. We request urgent disconnection and repair to prevent loss of life. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
