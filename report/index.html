Who do I report a community problem to?

Quick answer: Start with the level of government closest to the problem. Local streets, drains, refuse, markets and primary health centres are mainly Local Government matters. State roads, water supply and secondary schools belong to state ministries and agencies. Electricity goes to your distribution company first. Always write, date your letter and keep a stamped copy.
