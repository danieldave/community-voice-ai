Who do I report smoke, smell or noise pollution to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Write to the state environmental agency, describe what you see, smell or hear and when, and name the source only if you are sure of it.

Write toYour State Environmental Protection Agency or Ministry of Environment; the Local Government for local nuisances.
If nothing happens, go toThe national environmental standards agency for industrial pollution.

Collect this before you write

  • Dates and times it happens.
  • Photos or short videos.
  • How residents are affected.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

Residents of [area] have experienced [smoke or smell or noise] from [source, if known] since [date], mostly at [times]. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
