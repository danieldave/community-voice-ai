Who do I report a long power outage to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Complain first to your electricity distribution company in writing and keep the complaint number. If it is not resolved, escalate to the regulator's consumer forum office for your area.

Write toYour electricity distribution company (DisCo): start with its customer care or nearest business unit.
If nothing happens, go toIf the DisCo does not resolve it, the complaint can go to the electricity regulator's consumer forum office.

Collect this before you write

  • How long the outage has lasted.
  • The feeder or transformer name if you know it.
  • Your complaint or ticket number from the DisCo.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

We write on behalf of residents of [area], served by [transformer or feeder], who have been without electricity since [date]. A complaint was lodged on [date], reference [number]. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.

Replace the words in [brackets] with your own facts. Our AI can write the full letter from your answers, and you edit every word.
