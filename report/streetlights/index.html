Who do I report streetlights that do not work to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Write to the Local Government Council, list which poles are out, and explain the safety risk at night.

Write toThe Local Government Council, or the state agency that installed the lights.
If nothing happens, go toYour councillor or the Community Development Association.

Collect this before you write

  • Number of poles and which ones.
  • How long they have been off.
  • Any incidents at night.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

[Number] streetlights on [road] have not worked since [date]. The road is unsafe for residents returning at night. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
