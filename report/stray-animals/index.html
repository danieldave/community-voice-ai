Who do I report stray or dangerous animals to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Report stray dogs or dangerous animals to the Local Government veterinary or health office and ask for vaccination and control.

Write toThe Local Government health or veterinary department.
If nothing happens, go toThe State Ministry of Agriculture (veterinary services).

Collect this before you write

  • Where and when they are seen.
  • Any bites (numbers only).
  • Photos from a safe distance.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

A pack of stray dogs is regularly seen at [location]. [Number] residents have been bitten since [date]. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
