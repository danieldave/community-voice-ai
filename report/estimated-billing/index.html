Who do I report unfair estimated electricity bills to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Dispute the bill in writing with your DisCo, ask for a prepaid meter, and keep copies. If it is not settled, take the complaint to the regulator's consumer forum.

Write toYour electricity distribution company (DisCo), in writing.
If nothing happens, go toThe electricity regulator's consumer forum office.

Collect this before you write

  • Copies of the last few bills.
  • Hours of supply you actually receive.
  • Proof of any meter application.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

We write to dispute the estimated bills issued to residents of [area] for [months]. The amounts do not match the supply received, which averages [hours] a day. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
