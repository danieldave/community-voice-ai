Who do I report a bad or damaged road to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: First find out who owns the road. Local streets belong to the Local Government, state roads to the State Ministry of Works, and federal highways to the Federal Ministry of Works and FERMA. Then send a dated letter with photos and the exact location.

Write toYour Local Government Council for street and local roads; the State Ministry of Works for state roads; the Federal Ministry of Works or FERMA for federal highways.
If nothing happens, go toYour ward councillor, your State House of Assembly member, or the Public Complaints Commission.

Collect this before you write

  • Clear photos of the worst spots, with something for scale.
  • The road name, nearest landmark and length affected.
  • Who is affected: schools, clinics, markets, how many households.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

We, residents of [street or community], write to request the repair of [road name], between [landmark] and [landmark]. The road has been in this condition for [how long] and affects [who]. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.

Replace the words in [brackets] with your own facts. Our AI can write the full letter from your answers, and you edit every word.
