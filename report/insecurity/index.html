Who do I report theft or insecurity in the area to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Write to the Divisional Police Officer asking for patrols, and work with your community association. For a crime in progress, call 112; a letter is for patterns, not emergencies.

Write toThe Divisional Police Officer (DPO) of your nearest police station. In an emergency, call 112.
If nothing happens, go toThe Area Commander, your traditional ruler and the Community Development Association.

Collect this before you write

  • Dates and times of incidents (no accusations against named people).
  • Places that feel unsafe and why.
  • What the community is already doing.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

Residents of [area] have recorded [number] incidents of [type] between [dates], mostly at [times and places]. We request regular patrols. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.

Replace the words in [brackets] with your own facts. Our AI can write the full letter from your answers, and you edit every word.
