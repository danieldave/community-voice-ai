Who do I report uncollected refuse or an illegal dump to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Refuse collection is mainly a Local Government duty; some states also have a waste management authority. Write to them with photos and ask for a collection schedule.

Write toThe Local Government Council's environmental department, or your State Waste Management Authority where there is one.
If nothing happens, go toThe State Ministry of Environment.

Collect this before you write

  • Photos of the dump.
  • How long it has been there.
  • How close it is to homes, schools or water.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

Refuse has not been collected at [location] for [how long]. The heap now blocks [road or drain] and attracts rats and flies. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
