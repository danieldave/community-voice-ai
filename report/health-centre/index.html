Who do I report a health centre with no staff or drugs to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Primary health centres are run by the Local Government with the State Primary Health Care Board. Write to both, and involve the Ward Development Committee.

Write toThe Local Government health department or Primary Health Care Authority; the State Primary Health Care Board.
If nothing happens, go toThe Ward Development Committee and the State Ministry of Health.

Collect this before you write

  • Opening hours you actually observe.
  • What is missing: staff, drugs, power, water.
  • The nearest other clinic and how far it is.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

The health centre at [location] has been without [staff or drugs or power] since [date]. The nearest alternative is [distance] away. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
