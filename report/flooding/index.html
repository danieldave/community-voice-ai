Who do I report flooding to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Report flooding to the State Emergency Management Agency and your Local Government. After the emergency, write a letter asking for the cause (drains, culverts, river banks) to be fixed.

Write toYour State Emergency Management Agency (SEMA) and the Local Government Council. In danger, call 112.
If nothing happens, go toThe National Emergency Management Agency and the State Ministry of Environment.

Collect this before you write

  • Photos and dates of each flood.
  • Homes, farms and roads affected.
  • What you believe is causing it.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

Our community at [location] was flooded on [dates]. About [number] homes and [farms or shops] were affected. We request an assessment and a lasting solution. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.

Replace the words in [brackets] with your own facts. Our AI can write the full letter from your answers, and you edit every word.
