Who do I report no public toilets to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Ask the Local Government Council for public toilets at markets and motor parks, and propose how they could be maintained.

Write toThe Local Government Council.
If nothing happens, go toThe state water and sanitation agency.

Collect this before you write

  • Places with the greatest need.
  • How many people use the place daily.
  • What people do now.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

[Place] is used by about [number] people daily and has no public toilet. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.

Replace the words in [brackets] with your own facts. Our AI can write the full letter from your answers, and you edit every word.
