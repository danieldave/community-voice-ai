Who do I report not enough teachers to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Teacher posting is done by the state education bodies, so the letter should go through the principal and the PTA with the subjects and classes affected.

Write toThe principal, then SUBEB (primary) or the State Teaching Service Commission or Ministry of Education (secondary).
If nothing happens, go toThe PTA and the State House of Assembly member.

Collect this before you write

  • Subjects with no teacher.
  • How long the gap has lasted.
  • Classes and number of pupils affected.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

Our school has had no teacher for [subject] in [classes] since [date]. This affects [number] pupils preparing for examinations. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
