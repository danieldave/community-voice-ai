Who do I report an abandoned government project to in Nigeria?

Quick answer: Read the signboard to find the agency and contractor, then write to that agency. If no information is given, a Freedom of Information request can ask for the contract details.

Write toThe agency named on the project signboard. If there is none, ask the Local Government or use a Freedom of Information request.
If nothing happens, go toYour State or National Assembly member, and the Public Complaints Commission.

Collect this before you write

  • Photo of the signboard.
  • Photos of the site today.
  • When work stopped.
  • The exact thing you are asking for, in one sentence.

How your letter can begin

The [project] at [location], awarded by [agency on signboard], has been abandoned since [date]. We respectfully request that your office inspects the site and tells us what action will be taken.
