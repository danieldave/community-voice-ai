Questions about community complaint letters

Quick answer: find out which office is responsible, write a short polite letter (the problem, the effect, your request), keep an acknowledgement copy, and follow up after about two weeks.

How do I write a formal letter to a government office in Nigeria?

A formal letter has your address and date, the office's address, a salutation, a bold heading, three short paragraphs (the problem, the effect, the request), a polite close and your signature. Community Voice AI drafts this for you, and you edit every word. Read more

Who do I report a bad road to in Nigeria?

First find out who owns the road. Local streets belong to the Local Government, state roads to the State Ministry of Works, and federal highways to the Federal Ministry of Works and FERMA. Then send a dated letter with photos and the exact location. Read more

Who do I report a broken borehole or no water supply to?

For taps that have stopped running, write to the State Water Corporation. For a community borehole, write to the Local Government Council or the state rural water supply agency. In your letter, say exactly what is broken (pump, tank, generator, pipes), and offer what the community can contribute. Read more

Who do I complain to about a long power outage or estimated bill?

Complain first to your electricity distribution company in writing and keep the complaint number. If it is not resolved, escalate to the regulator's consumer forum office for your area. Read more

Who is responsible for refuse collection and blocked drains?

Refuse collection is mainly a Local Government duty; some states also have a waste management authority. Write to them with photos and ask for a collection schedule. For blocked drains, write to the Local Government Council before the rains. Read more

How do I prove that my letter was received?

Take two copies. Ask the receiving officer to stamp, sign and date your copy. That "acknowledgement copy" is your proof. Read more

What do I do if nobody replies to my letter?

Wait about two weeks, then send a short follow-up that quotes the date and reference of the first letter. If there is still no reply, go one level higher and copy the first office. Our letter tracker reminds you after 14 days and drafts the follow-up. Read more

How do I write a community petition with signatures?

A petition is a formal letter signed by many people. Keep the request to one clear sentence and attach a signature sheet with names, streets and signatures. Read more

How do I make a Freedom of Information (FOI) request in Nigeria?

Under the Freedom of Information Act, anyone can write to a public institution asking for records, such as a project's contract sum. You do not have to say why, and the law gives the institution seven days to respond. Read more

How can students report a problem in their school or community?

Students can write a respectful letter through the class teacher, student council or principal, describe the problem with facts, and suggest a solution. Read more

What should I do in an emergency?

A letter is not for emergencies. Call 112, which is free on every network. If it does not connect, go to the nearest police station, fire station or hospital. Read more
