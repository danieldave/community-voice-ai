📬 My Letters

Many letters to government are never followed up, so nothing happens. Here you can see every letter you sent, how many days have passed, and write a polite follow-up after 14 days. Your letters are saved on this phone only.

Why follow up?

  • Offices receive many letters. A follow-up shows the community is still waiting and is organised.
  • Mention the date of your first letter and any reference number. Keep copies of everything.
  • After two follow-ups with no answer, ask your Community Development Association (CDA) or traditional ruler to deliver the next one in person.
