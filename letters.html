📬 My Letters

Many letters to government are never followed up, so nothing happens. Here you can see every letter you sent, its status (sent, acknowledged, resolved), add a reminder to your calendar, write a polite follow-up in one tap, or turn the letter into a petition. Your letters are saved on this phone only.

Not sure? See who to send it to.

Why follow up?

  • Offices receive many letters. A follow-up shows the community is still waiting and is organised.
  • Mention the date of your first letter and any reference number. Keep copies of everything.
  • After two follow-ups with no answer, ask your Community Development Association (CDA) or traditional ruler to deliver the next one in person.
  • Many voices are stronger than one: ✍️ Make it a petition lets neighbours add their first name and area, and gives you a signature list (PDF) to attach.

📅 Calendar reminder downloads a small .ics file. Open it and your phone's calendar adds the reminder. It stays on your phone; we never see it.
