How to write a letter to the State Ministry of Works

Quick answer: The State Ministry of Works builds and maintains state roads, bridges and public buildings. Address the letter to "The Honourable Commissioner for Works" and deliver it to the ministry at the state secretariat.

What this office handles

  • State roads
  • Bridges and culverts on state roads
  • Road drainage

How to reach them

Address the letter to "The Honourable Commissioner for Works" and deliver it to the ministry at the state secretariat.

Problems to take to this office

  • A bad or damaged road
  • Potholes
  • A dangerous road crossing near a school
  • A broken bridge or culvert
