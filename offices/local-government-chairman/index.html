How to write a letter to the Local Government Chairman

Quick answer: The Local Government Chairman leads your Local Government Council, the level of government closest to you. Address the letter to "The Executive Chairman, [Name] Local Government", deliver it to the council secretariat, and ask for a stamped, dated copy.

What this office handles

  • Local streets and drains
  • Refuse collection
  • Markets and motor parks
  • Primary health centres (with the state)
  • Streetlights

How to reach them

Address the letter to "The Executive Chairman, [Name] Local Government", deliver it to the council secretariat, and ask for a stamped, dated copy.

Problems to take to this office

  • A bad or damaged road
  • Potholes
  • A broken community borehole
  • Streetlights that do not work
  • Fallen or dangerous electric cables
  • Uncollected refuse or an illegal dump
  • Blocked drainage or gutters
  • Flooding
