How to write a letter to a traditional ruler

Quick answer: A traditional ruler (Oba, Emir, Obi, Baale or village head) is the custodian of the community and has the ear of government. Go through the palace secretary or a chief, follow local custom, and bring the letter in person with community elders.

What this office handles

  • Community disputes
  • Land matters
  • Calling the community together
  • Speaking to government for the community

How to reach them

Go through the palace secretary or a chief, follow local custom, and bring the letter in person with community elders.

Problems to take to this office

  • Illegal tree cutting or bush burning
  • Theft or insecurity in the area
