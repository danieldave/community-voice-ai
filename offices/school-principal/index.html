How to write a letter to your school principal

Quick answer: The principal runs the school day to day and speaks for it to the education authorities. Write a respectful letter through your class teacher or the student council, and suggest a solution.

What this office handles

  • School rules and safety
  • Requests to the ministry or board
  • Working with the PTA

How to reach them

Write a respectful letter through your class teacher or the student council, and suggest a solution.

Problems to take to this office

  • A damaged school building
  • No toilets or water in school
  • Not enough desks or chairs
  • Not enough teachers
