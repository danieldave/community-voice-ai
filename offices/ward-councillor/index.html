How to write a letter to your ward councillor

Quick answer: A councillor is elected to represent your ward in the Local Government Council. Ask at the council secretariat for your ward councillor and hand the letter to their office.

What this office handles

  • Raising ward problems in council
  • Following up on council projects
  • Linking residents to the Chairman

How to reach them

Ask at the council secretariat for your ward councillor and hand the letter to their office.

Problems to take to this office

  • A bad or damaged road
  • Potholes
  • No public water supply
  • A broken community borehole
  • Streetlights that do not work
  • No playground, library or youth centre
