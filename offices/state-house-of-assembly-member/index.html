How to write a letter to the State House of Assembly member

Quick answer: Your Assembly member represents your constituency in making state laws and can raise your problem with state ministries. Write to "The Honourable Member representing [constituency]" at the constituency office or the House of Assembly complex.

What this office handles

  • Raising matters in the House
  • Constituency projects
  • Oversight of state agencies

How to reach them

Write to "The Honourable Member representing [constituency]" at the constituency office or the House of Assembly complex.

Problems to take to this office

  • A bad or damaged road
  • Potholes
  • Gully erosion
  • Not enough teachers
  • An abandoned government project
  • No playground, library or youth centre
