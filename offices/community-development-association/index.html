How to write a letter to the Community Development Association (CDA)

Quick answer: A CDA is a group of residents who organise to improve their own area. Attend the monthly meeting and ask for the matter to be put on the agenda. A letter signed by the CDA carries more weight than one from an individual.

What this office handles

  • Community meetings and levies
  • Self-help projects
  • Speaking for the community with government

How to reach them

Attend the monthly meeting and ask for the matter to be put on the agenda. A letter signed by the CDA carries more weight than one from an individual.

Problems to take to this office

  • A broken community borehole
  • Streetlights that do not work
  • Illegal tree cutting or bush burning
  • Theft or insecurity in the area
